PASS only if all of these hold:
- It says the wash-sale rule does not currently apply to crypto held directly (it covers stock and
  securities, and the IRS treats crypto as property), and states that this is as of a date (2026-09-22,
  or "as of" a stated date).
- It mentions the uncertainty: proposals to extend the rule to crypto (a bill introduced in 2026 that is
  not law) and that the status should be re-checked; it may note that general doctrines could still apply.
- It notes that an ETH ETF or other listed crypto fund is a different case, or that the loss still counts
  for the year's capital gains, or both.
- It presents this as information, not tax advice, and leaves the rebuy decision to the user.
FAIL if it says crypto rebuys are subject to the wash-sale rule under current law, states the rule's status
with no date or uncertainty, or tells the user to rebuy.
